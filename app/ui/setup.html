First run

Photo Pigeon

A pigeon that watches a folder and carries every new photo home to Google Photos.

Setup is five screens in your browser, most of them one button. You end up with your own Google Cloud project, which means your own daily upload quota that nobody else shares, and credentials that live only on this machine.

Nothing is ever deleted. Photo Pigeon uploads, in one direction, and that is the whole of it. It cannot read your existing library and it cannot remove anything from it. There is no code in it that could.

Uploads are original quality and count against your Google storage. Storage Saver does not apply.

Start with Windows

The blue box Windows showed

If Windows put up a blue box before this app opened, that was SmartScreen noticing that Photo Pigeon is not signed by a big publisher yet, and More info then Run anyway is the way through it.

Drag the downloaded JSON file here

Watching your Downloads folder. If the file lands there, Photo Pigeon picks it up on its own.

The page Google shows once

The first time you sign in, Google shows a full page saying it has not verified this app, and the way through is Advanced and then the link underneath it.

Your browser has the sign in now. Photo Pigeon waits here until it is finished, and then it starts watching.

Photo Pigeon is set up and signed in.

Anything already in your watched folders goes on the first pass. The pigeon lives in the notification area.

Uploads are original quality and count against your Google storage. Storage Saver does not apply.

Where the pigeon went

Windows 11 tucks new tray icons into the hidden flyout, so click the small arrow at the left of the clock and drag the pigeon down onto the bar to keep it in sight.
